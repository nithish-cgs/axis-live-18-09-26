Frequently Asked Questions

A trading account is where one can buy or sell shares in a stock market. It is linked with a demat account and bank account of an investor. The shares get credited in the the demat account on settlement day and the buy transaction valuegets deducted from the bank account. Each trading account has a unique trading ID which is utilised to perform online transactions. A Demat Account or Dematerialised Account provides the facility of holding shares in an electronic format. Shares are held in a Demat Account, thus, facilitating easy trade for the users. A Demat Account holds the investments an individual makes in shares, securities, exchange-traded funds, bonds and mutual funds in one place.

The difference between a Demat and Trading account is that a Demat account holds shares and securities in the Digital Mode, while a trading account provides the interface to buy and sell shares in the stock market. Despite the different roles they play, a Demat and Trading Account work together to make online trading possible.

Here’s a list of documents that are required to open a trading account –

  1. Identity proof - PAN card
  2. Signature on a white paper
  3. Live Photo
  4. Personalized Cancelled Cheque
  5. Address proof (Voter ID card, Passport, Aadhaar card or Driving License)
  6. Income proof – Activation of any Derivatives
    7. (Any one: copy of ITR, net worth certificate, salary proof, Annual accounts, Demat Account Holding statement) - *Last 6 months
  7. Email Id, Mobile number, Aadhar card
*Note:- Only Individual Accounts can be opened digitally. For Non Individual category, please apply through Physical Form.
